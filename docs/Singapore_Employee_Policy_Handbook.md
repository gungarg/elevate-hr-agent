# Altostrat Employee Policy Handbook & Guidelines (Singapore Region)

## Section 1.1: Outpatient & Hospitalization Sick Leave (Singapore)
- **Outpatient Sick Leave:** Employees receive 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave per calendar year, paid at 100% base salary.
- **Documentation Rule:** Sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a certified Medical Certificate (MC) from a registered medical practitioner submitted to People Operations within 48 hours.
- **Hospitalization Leave:** 46 work days of paid hospitalization leave per calendar year for inpatient hospital stays and registered day surgeries.

## Section 1.2: Paid Vacation Leave (Singapore)
- **Accrual Tier Matrix:**
  - 1 to 6 years of service: 20 working days per year.
  - 7 to 10 years of service: 21 working days per year.
  - 11+ years of service: 22 working days per year.
- **Booking Rule:** Vacation requests must be submitted at least 15 days in advance in WorkWeek and approved by your reporting line manager.
- **Carryover:** Up to 5 unused vacation days may carry over into the following calendar year, expiring after 12 months.

## Section 1.3: Parental & Childcare Leave (Singapore)
- **Maternity Leave:** 16 weeks of paid maternity leave for eligible female employees under the Singapore Child Development Co-Savings Act.
- **Paternity Leave:** 4 weeks of government-paid paternity leave for eligible male employees.
- **Childcare Leave:** 6 days of paid childcare leave per year for parents with Singapore Citizen children aged under 7 years.

## Section 3.1: Bereavement & Compassionate Leave
- **Duration:** Up to 4 weeks (20 work days) of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- **Flexibility:** May be taken continuously or in intermittent blocks within 12 months of the loss.

## Section 4.3: Host Gifts & Entertainment Guidelines
- **Host Gifts:** When staying with friends or family during business travel in lieu of commercial hotel lodging, employees may expense non-cash host gifts up to US $50 per day.
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Cash, gift cards, or monetary vouchers are strictly prohibited under company anti-bribery and expense compliance policies.

## Section 4.4: Business Travel Meals & Incidental Expenses
- **Daily Meal Cap:** Actual incurred meal expenses are reimbursable up to US $120 per day on global business trips.
- **Policy Condition:** All expense reimbursements require itemized receipts; this is an expense reimbursement limit, not an automatic per diem allowance.

## Section 5.4: Remote Work Home Office Equipment Allowance
- **Equipment Allowance:** Full-time remote and designated hybrid employees are eligible for a $500 USD allowance for ergonomic home office equipment (monitors, displays, ergonomic chairs, desks, and peripherals).
- **Procurement Workflow:** Equipment requisitions must be submitted through ServiceImmediately under Ticket Category 'Facilities' with a verified remote shipping address.

## Section 5.5: Relocation Allowance, Badging & ITSM Ticket Lifecycle
- **Relocation Allowance:** Employees transferring to international offices (such as London HQ) receive up to a $10,000 USD relocation allowance. Transferring employees must open a Facilities ticket (Priority: '3 - Moderate') for destination building badging.
- **ITSM Ticket State Machine:** All service desk tickets must progress sequentially through standard operational states (New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed). Bypassing intermediate states (e.g. New directly to Closed) or modifying Closed tickets is strictly prohibited.
